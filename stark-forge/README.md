# StarkForge - Decentralized Media Licensing Marketplace

## Project Description

StarkForge revolutionizes the intellectual property landscape by creating a decentralized marketplace where creators can monetize their media assets through programmable licensing agreements. Built on the Stacks blockchain, the platform addresses critical pain points in traditional media licensing including lack of transparency, complex rights management, and unfair revenue distribution. By introducing Dynamic License NFTs (DL-NFTs), creators gain unprecedented control over their intellectual property with granular permissions encoded directly into smart contracts.

The platform leverages zero-knowledge proofs to maintain content authenticity and usage compliance while protecting sensitive creator metadata. Through its innovative Content Provenance Graph, StarkForge automatically tracks derivative works and remix chains, ensuring original creators receive fair compensation when their work serves as foundation material. This creates a transparent, trustless ecosystem where licensing agreements execute automatically and revenue distribution happens seamlessly.

## Key Features

• **Dynamic License NFTs (DL-NFTs)** - Smart contract-powered licenses with customizable parameters including usage duration, geographic restrictions, commercial rights, and dynamic pricing that adjusts based on real-time demand metrics

• **Zero-Knowledge Content Verification** - Advanced cryptographic proofs verify media authenticity and monitor usage compliance without exposing proprietary algorithms or sensitive creator metadata

• **Content Provenance Graph** - Sophisticated tracking system that maps derivative works and remix chains, automatically distributing royalties to original creators in complex multi-layer licensing scenarios

• **AI-Powered License Matching Engine** - Intelligent system that connects content seekers with appropriate media assets based on budget constraints, intended usage, and specific rights requirements

• **Reputation Staking System** - Economic incentive mechanism where creators stake tokens on content authenticity, with slashing penalties for copyright violations, promoting honest behavior and platform integrity

• **Automated Rights Management** - Smart contracts handle the complete licensing lifecycle including agreement execution, payment processing in stablecoins or native tokens, and compliance monitoring through on-chain verification

• **Integrated Creator Tools** - Comprehensive suite including watermarking capabilities, content fingerprinting technology, and decentralized arbitration for automated DMCA-style dispute resolution

## Revenue Model

The platform maintains economic sustainability through transaction fees, premium creator toolsets, enterprise API access, and a secondary marketplace for trading license derivatives, all while prioritizing creator empowerment and fair compensation.
